How to Talk to Clients About Color When You're Colorblind

June 4, 2025

Becoming a florist is a rewarding career, but it can be challenging for those who are colorblind. Communicating effectively with clients about color choices is crucial. Here are some tips on how to talk to clients about color when you're colorblind:

  1. Acknowledge Your Colorblindness: Be open about your colorblindness with your clients. This transparency builds trust and helps them understand your perspective.
  2. Focus on Function Over Feel: Clients don't just want color - they want what color does: evoke emotion, guide attention, signal interactivity. Talk in those terms: "This color palette feels warm and welcoming."
  3. Use Tools to Guide the Conversation: There are many tooks that let you work confidently with color. Color pickers with names and codes include Adobe Color, Coolers, and Figma. Contrast checkers include WebAIM and Stark. Stimulaters allow you to preview your work as others see it such as Cobli and Sim Daltonism. Palette generators are available with accessible presents.
  4. Use Naming Systems, Not Just Visual Description: Clients will often say "can you make that more blue?" Instead, guide them to talk in more specific terms such as hex codes, Pantone names, or style tokens. Further, describe colors functionally with words such as "highlight," "neutral," "accent".
  5. Collaborate With a Trusted Second pair of Eyes: If the project heavily depends on color nuance build in a a checkpoing: "For final polishing, I review colors with a trusted visual designer to make sure everything aligns with the intent." This is professional, not a crutch - all designers get feedback on color.
