Top Apps and Tools for Colorblind Florists

June 4, 2025

Apps and tools can significantly enhance the workflow of colorblind florists. In this post, we will explore some of the best applications and tools available that can help colorblind florists in their daily tasks, from selecting the right colors to managing their floral arrangements. Here is a list of some of the most useful apps and tools for colorblind florists:

Color Blind Pal

This app identifies colors in real-time and helps users ses colors more clearly by manipulating the colors displayed on their device's screen, or by analyzing colors in images. Some features included:
  • Simulating Color Blindness
  • Color Identification
  • Color Filters
  • Color Palette Analysis
  • Image Inspection

    • ColorADD

    This app creates a solution to provide independence and autonomy in the day-to-day life =of the colorblind, complementing the presence of the ColorADD code in proucts and services. To use this app, the user simply points the device's camer at the surface he wants to identify and at the top of the screen the color name appears with the corresponding ColorADD symbol. Features include:
    • Color detection and identification in real time
    • Zoom, flip, and flashlight control
    • Intelligent color stabilizer
    • Color detection and identification in images from the phone gallery, with the ability to identify up to 10 colors at the same time
    • Possibility of saving and sharing images after color identification
